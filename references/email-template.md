# Client email template

Reusable structure for update emails to a client after a security or functionality fix. Tone: professional but warm, not alarmist - the goal is reassurance plus clarity, never to make a routine fix sound like a crisis. Pull the content from the current entry in the project's client-facing log (see project config) and rewrite it in plain sentences; don't paste technical bullet points verbatim.

---

**From:** [your sending address]
**To:** [client contact address(es), from the project config]

**Subject:** Website update - [short label, e.g. "Security update completed" or "Content update in progress"]

Hi [client contact name],

Quick update on your website:

[1-2 sentences on what happened, in plain language, drawn from the current client-log entry]

[If this was a security/stability fix: make clear it was routine/precautionary maintenance, not a sign of an active problem - no evidence of an actual attack or data exposure, and the site remained reachable throughout]

[If you need a decision or input from the client: say exactly what's needed and by when, with a link to a preview if there is one]

Feel free to reach out anytime with questions.

Best regards,
[your name / signature]

---

## Example, filled in

**From:** agency@example.com
**To:** owner@clientcompany.com

**Subject:** Website update - Security update completed

Hi Alex,

An automated security scan flagged a few vulnerabilities in some of the technical components behind your website. I addressed all of them the same day, and a follow-up scan confirms: no vulnerabilities remain.

These were precautionary technical updates - there was no indication at any point of an actual attack or data exposure, and the site stayed fully reachable throughout. There's nothing you need to do on your end.

Feel free to reach out anytime with questions.

Best regards,
Jordan
